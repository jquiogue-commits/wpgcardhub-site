Questions before launch

The short version

Is WPG CardHub free?

Yes. Browsing shows, saving favorites, RSVPing, and joining the discussion are all free for everyone.

Do I need an account?

No — browsing and RSVPing ("I'm Going") work without signing in. An account is only needed for app owners who manage the app.

What devices will it support?

iPhone, running iOS 17 or later.
